1

An organizer creates the group

Who: a Dhanvi-approved organizer, or the Dhanvi team itself

The organizer sets the few numbers that define the whole group: the group value (the pool paid out each month), the number of members, the type (random draw or auction) and the three dates that repeat every month — contribution day, selection day, payout day.

Dhanvi calculates the rest and writes it into a rule sheet that never changes once members are approved.

Organizers apply first and are approved by Dhanvi before they can publish a group. A member browsing groups always sees who runs it.

Members find the group and apply

Who: anyone with a Dhanvi account

Published groups appear in Browse groups with the value, monthly amount, duration and organizer shown up front. Applying costs nothing — no money moves at this stage.

The organizer reviews each application and approves or declines it with a reason. Approved members hold a numbered position in the group.

3

Everyone accepts the same rules, then the group starts

Who: every approved member, then the organizer

Once all 20 positions are approved, each member reads and accepts the rule sheet: the amount, the dates, how the winner is chosen and when the group starts. Dhanvi keeps a fingerprint of the exact version each person accepted.

When everyone has accepted, the organizer confirms the group is ready and starts it on the start date. From that moment the rules are locked — nobody, including the organizer, can change them.

4

Every month, every member contributes

Who: all 20 members · tracked by the organizer or paid in-app

Each month is a cycle. Every member owes the same ₹25,000 by the due date. Their Dhanvi home screen shows exactly one thing to do: Cycle 3 contribution · ₹25,000 due · Pay now.

Groups run by Dhanvi collect the money online through Razorpay, so the payment confirms itself. Organizer-run groups collect outside the app and the organizer records each payment against the member — with a reference, visible to that member.

The month cannot move on until every contribution is in. Members who have paid see "waiting for 3 other members", not a mystery.

5

The pool goes to one member — chosen fairly

Who: decided by the group type set in step 1

A member who has already received the pool keeps contributing but is not eligible again, so over the full run everyone gets it exactly once. There are two ways to decide whose turn it is:

Random draw

Luck, provably fair

Dhanvi draws one eligible member and publishes the proof. Anyone in the group can re-run the verification and get the same result.

Winner receives the full ₹5,00,000.

Auction

Whoever needs it most, bids

Eligible members offer a discount — how much less than the full pool they will accept. The highest discount wins. The discount is shared back with the other members as a benefit.

Winner receives ₹5,00,000 minus their discount.

6

The winner is paid, and everyone can see it

Who: Dhanvi processes the transfer to the winner's verified bank account

The winner adds a bank account once (name, account number, IFSC), and Dhanvi approves the destination before any transfer. The payout then moves through clear stages the member can follow on their phone: Approved → Processing → Paid, with the bank account shown as "ending 1234".

Every payout, contribution and auction benefit is posted to a double-entry ledger, so the group's money always adds up and every rupee has a trail.

7

Next month, the cycle repeats — until everyone has had a turn

Who: everyone, for 20 months

Cycle 4 opens the moment cycle 3's payout settles. The same ₹25,000 falls due, a new winner is chosen from the 17 members still waiting, and so on. After 20 cycles, every member has paid ₹5,00,000 in total and received the pool once. The group is marked complete.

Someone who wins early gets money sooner; someone who wins late has, in an auction group, collected benefits along the way. The rules the group agreed to in step 3 decide which — never a person after the fact.

  • Approves applications

Dhanvi

Keeps it honest

  • Verifies organizers
  • Runs platform groups with online payments
  • Publishes draw proofs, closes auctions
  • Approves bank accounts and executes payouts
  • Keeps the ledger and audit trail

What you can say with confidence

  • The monthly amount and the dates are fixed before the first rupee is paid.
  • Every member receives the pool exactly once.
  • Random draws come with a proof anyone can re-check.
  • Auction discounts flow back to the other members, not to the organizer.
  • Payouts go only to a bank account Dhanvi has approved.
  • Every payment and payout has a record the member can see.
  • Dhanvi does not promise returns — it organizes savings, transparently.
  • Organizers are verified people, not anonymous accounts.
